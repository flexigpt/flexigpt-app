# Professional writing

- Write in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: Aim for 80% of compliance that will enforce controlled clarity, natural phrasing, exact technical terms.
- Address a competent peer: professional, neutral, respectful, direct.
- Avoid "Do this", "Don't do this", influencer language.
- Lead with the result. Explain mechanisms, evidence, decisions, and consequences.
- Use diagrams when they clarify relationships.
